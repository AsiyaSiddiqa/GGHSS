Message of the Principal

It is a matter of great honor and privilege to lead Govt. Girls Higher Secondary School, Mianwal Ranjha. Our institution is deeply committed to empowering young women through quality education, strong moral values, and academic excellence.

We strive to create a safe, supportive, and innovative learning environment where every student is encouraged to explore her potential and excel in her academic journey. With dedicated faculty, modern facilities, and a legacy of excellent academic results, our focus remains on nurturing confident, skilled, and responsible future leaders.

We welcome our students to embark on a journey of growth, character building, and success with us.

Principal. Ms Farwa Nawaz
